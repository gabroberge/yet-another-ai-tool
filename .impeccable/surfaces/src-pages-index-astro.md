Scope: YAAT marketing landing page (Persuade). Related: Factory, Portfolio, Telemetry app screens (Operate) inherit the same world.

Audience/job: fictional buyers (founders, VPs Eng, investors) addressed in-world; real visitors are tech-literate readers. Job: understand YAAT in seconds, manufacture a startup from an existing technology right in the hero, then continue in the Factory.

## Direction contract

THESIS: YAAT is a packaging company for existing technology. Every venture ships as a carton that grows with each agent, layer, and claim, while its Contents panel always names the same underlying library, API, tool, or algorithm. Refuses the dark gradient AI launch page with a floating product window.

OWN-WORLD: cool coated-board white ground, ultramarine #3A2BFF owning whole panels, magenta die-lines and crease marks for print structure only, black ink, yellow claim seals earned by state. Archivo across widths: expanded black for brand and claims, narrow bold for contents labels, regular for body. Shelf-edge price labels, barcodes, nutrition-style Contents panels. Sharp carton corners.

STORY: visitor sees a credible platform, manufactures a venture from a real-sounding technology in the hero, watches the packaging line grow one venture step by step, sees the shelf of what others built, reads pricing on shelf labels, opens the Factory.

FIRST VIEWPORT: unfolded carton net across the width. Top flap: technology picker chips and Manufacture button (primary action). Front panel, ultramarine, headline "Every capability deserves a company." at display scale with one sentence. Left side panel: live Contents label. Right: the assembled venture box on a shelf edge with its shelf label (valuation, price). Nav above the net with Open the Factory.

FORM: Shelf-Ready, roll position 3 of 7, seed:4580afbea2ae9757. Signature interaction: carton grows and claim seals stamp on as layers are added; valuation and price cascade digit by digit; Contents panel never changes size. Internal mechanic borrowed from The Stack: an exploded architecture view inside each venture, not the site identity.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
